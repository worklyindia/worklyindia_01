Information we collect

  • Account details: name, email, phone number, profile photo.

  • To create and manage your account and show relevant jobs or candidates.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. Job seeker profiles are shared with employers when you apply. We use trusted service providers (hosting, messaging, payments) only to run the service.

Retention and your rights

We keep data while your account is active. You may access, correct or delete your data at any time. See Data Deletion.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and access is restricted.
