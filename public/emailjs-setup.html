📧 EmailJS Setup Guide

Follow this guide to set up EmailJS for client-side email sending without a backend server.

Step 1: Create EmailJS Account

  1. Go to EmailJS.com
  2. Click "Sign Up" and create a free account
  3. Verify your email address

Step 2: Add Email Service

  1. Go to "Email Services" in your EmailJS dashboard
  2. Click "Add New Service"
  3. Choose your email provider (Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo, etc.)
  4. Follow the setup instructions for your chosen provider
  5. Note down your Service ID
⚠️ Important: For Gmail, you'll need to use App Passwords, not your regular password.

Step 3: Create Email Template

  1. Go to "Email Templates" in your dashboard
  2. Click "Create New Template"
  3. Use this template structure:
Subject: {{subject}}

Body:
From: {{from_name}} ({{from_email}})
To: {{to_email}}

Message:
{{message}}
  1. Save the template and note down your Template ID

Step 4: Get Public Key

  1. Go to "Account" → "General"
  2. Find your Public Key
  3. Copy this key

Step 5: Add EmailJS Script

Add this script tag to your HTML <head> section:

<script src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/@emailjs/browser@4/dist/email.min.js"></script>

Step 6: Configure in Email Management Page

  1. Go to /email-management on your website
  2. In the "Setup" tab, enter:
    • Public Key: From Step 4
    • Service ID: From Step 2
    • Template ID: From Step 3
    • From Name: Your name or company
    • From Email: Your email address
  3. Click "Save Configuration"
✅ Success! You can now send emails directly from your website without a backend server!

Troubleshooting

  • Emails not sending: Check console for errors and verify all IDs are correct
  • Gmail issues: Make sure you're using App Passwords, not regular password
  • Template errors: Ensure template variables match the ones used in the code
  • CORS errors: EmailJS handles CORS automatically, but check your browser console

EmailJS Free Plan Limits

  • 200 emails per month
  • Basic email templates
  • Community support

For higher volume, consider upgrading to a paid plan.
